# Measured items list

Three closing types exist: **daily**, **weekly**, **monthly**.

- Daily is a subset of weekly (grey rows on the "Napi leltár" sheet).
- Weekly = every row on the "Napi leltár" sheet.
- Monthly ("Havi Leltár" sheet) is a **superset** of weekly: it repeats the ice
  creams / frozen fruits / fresh produce and adds the whole shop stock —
  display case, bars, packaging & cleaning (DECOS), other ingredients, coffee.

Under each category the subsections are **additive**: "Measured weekly" lists
what weekly adds on top of daily, "Measured monthly" what monthly adds on top
of weekly.
